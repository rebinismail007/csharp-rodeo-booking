# Study-source connection

The supplied `Frisør Virksomhed - Business Models` PDF contains the Rodeo business case and customer/admin user stories. Pages 14–19 discuss treatment selection, choosing a date/time, confirming a booking and viewing appointments. The new implementation covers a small subset of those ideas and adds explicit duration/overlap rules and tests.

This is a new reconstruction, not a recovered copy of the original group application. The real salon's accounts, branding assets, external integrations and customer records are not part of this demonstration.
